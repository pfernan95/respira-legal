Respira — Delete your account

You can delete your Respira account and all of its data at any time.

From the app

Open Respira and go to Profile → About Respira → Delete account. You will be asked to confirm. The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email respiraapp.info@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask for deletion. We will verify it is you and confirm when it is done.

What gets deleted

Your account, your profile, your saved locations, your trips, your notification settings and your entire symptom diary are removed from our servers.

Pseudonymised analytics events already collected are retained by our analytics provider under its own schedule; once your account is gone they are no longer linked to your identity. If you had turned analytics off in the app, there are none.

Your subscription is separate. Deleting your account does not cancel a Respira Pro subscription — that is managed by Apple. Cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Respira before deleting, or it will keep renewing.
